# Run sheet: walking the class through Unseen Above (Nov 18)

About 22 minutes: 18 on screen, 4 spare for talk and questions. The route, the keys, and where things can go wrong.
Talking points are yours: each stop has a blank line for them.

## Before class (30 minutes early)

- [ ] Copy `game/dist/local.html` and `game/dist/phaser.min.js` into one folder on the desktop, and the same two files onto a USB stick. Double-click `local.html`: it runs with no network. For the secret TV, also copy `game/dist/trailer.mp4` and `game/dist/trailer.html` into the same folder (without the mp4 the TV plays the in-engine trailer instead; it may need a click on Play inside the set when opened from a file).
- [ ] Open it in Chrome on the presenting laptop. Use the local copy, not the web link, unless the network is good. (GitHub Pages can take 10 minutes to show a new build.)
- [ ] On the title: **Presenter** on. That sets the mode to IMMUNE (hazards still play out, then she comes back at the last tape mark with nothing lost), turns flashing down, stops the shake, and sets the music to 2 of 5 and the effects to 4 of 5.
- [ ] On the title: **New game**, twice (it asks). This clears your rehearsal: lines you heard, how-to slips, bodies. Without it some lines stay silent in class.
- [ ] Plug in the projector. Check where the sound goes (HDMI sound may go to a weak projector speaker). Bring a small speaker.
- [ ] Press one key before you start talking: browsers start sound only after a key or a click.
- [ ] Check the levels in the show, not on the title: press **0**, then **P**: music 2, effects 4. **P** again. Turn effects up if the room is big; the clicks and the strike carry the story.
- [ ] **U** for full screen (or Full screen on the title). Esc leaves full screen, so in full screen use **P** for the menu.
- [ ] Turn on Do Not Disturb, close chat apps and extra tabs (a notification or focus change opens the pause menu on screen). Plug in power.
- [ ] Set the display to 1920x1080 when mirroring, then **U**: the picture should fill the screen.
- [ ] Decide which ending you show, and whether to give a content note first (the default ending is the vest).
- [ ] Darken the room. Check the bedroom (key **6**): it is lit only in red and projectors wash out dark scenes. Projector on a game or cinema mode.
- [ ] Have the screen recording of a full run ready as a fallback (in the `Sync` folder with the offline copy). If the game fails, play it and talk over it.
- [ ] Rehearse the route once on this laptop and browser. Then **P**, **Title screen**, **New game** again. The title clears the address, so **Begin** starts at the opening.

## Keys for tonight

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **0** to **9** | Jump to a part, with its title card: 0 opening, 1 ep 1, 2 satellite 1, 3 ep 2, 4 satellite 2, 5 ep 3, 6 ep 4, 7 kids' room, 8 Moon, 9 backstage |
| **H** | Hold the picture to talk. No menu over it, sound ducks. H again goes on |
| **N** | Skip ahead one section per press (or solves a satellite screen). An episode has 4 to 7 sections, so keep pressing. In presenter mode it always works |
| **B** | Back, N's opposite (works wherever N does). In an episode: the section behind her; at the first one, the part before (from its start). A line or a beat (the opening, the bedroom talk, the kids' room, the playroom, an ending's last shot): that line again; pressed at its start, the one before. A satellite screen: the puzzle before, then the request form, then the part before. Never costs a life or a take |
| **I** | The mode: NORMAL (5 lives), ENDLESS (she dies, lives never run out), IMMUNE (she comes back at the mark, nothing lost). The strip's right end says which |
| **W** | Doors, every kind. J never opens a door |
| **Backspace** | Start this take again |
| **Enter** | Next line (the bedroom, backstage and the playroom wait for it) |
| **P** | Pause menu (presenter, big captions, music and effects levels, title screen) |
| **M** | Mute at once (works while held with H too) |
| **U** | Full screen on and off |
| **F5** (Mac: **Cmd+R**) | A refresh goes straight back into the part you were in (presenter mode) |

On a Mac, **Backspace** is the **delete** key.

Playroom reply to ending: **Who picks the lights?** gives THE VEST. **Yes.** gives ROUND TWO. **Turn it around.** gives LOOK BACK (the watchers made visible: the remedy beat).

Endings and give-up mode: title screen, **Chapters**, right column. In presenter mode all five endings are open there.

The paper's books: one in each episode (1, 3, 5, 6, 8), a small book on the floor with a glint, a step behind where she starts (walk left; in the east wing, at the landing's west wall). **J** on it opens that Part's EXHIBIT slip; **R** opens that Part of the paper in a new tab (the game pauses when its tab loses focus: **P** to go on), **J** closes. The ending screen counts BOOKS n/5. The paper itself: **Paper** on the title, **PAPER** on the ending screen, or **R** on How the game argues. The paper's excerpts come from paper.md when the game is built: run `npm run build` after editing it.

The secret TV (ep 1, key **1**): beside the book, behind where she starts (walk left, past the dead tree). **J** on it (or a click) turns it on: the old set fills the picture and the trailer plays in its screen, with sound; the game waits and its music stops. **Space** pauses and goes on, **A** and **D** (or the two small knobs) go back or on 10 s, **J**, **Esc** or the power knob turn it off. It clicks off by itself at the end. **U** still does full screen with it up.

The picture fills the window now. The small **keys** toggle at the right end of the hint line folds the key bar away for more picture (in full screen it starts folded).

## If something goes wrong

- Stuck or confused: **N**. Wrong part: the number key. A part looks broken: **Backspace**, then the number key.
- Frozen or black: **F5**. In presenter mode it comes straight back into the same part (press **U** again for full screen).
- Sound gone: **M** (muted?), then the pause menu levels, then the laptop's output device.
- Game will not load: the USB copy. Then the recording.
- A how-to slip pops up: any key closes it. A number key closes it and jumps in one press.
- Back to the title (**P**, **Title screen**): **Continue** goes back to the part you were in; **From the start** begins at the opening.
