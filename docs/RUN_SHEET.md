# Run sheet: walking the class through Unseen Above (Nov 18)

About 22 minutes: 18 on screen, 4 spare for talk and questions. The route, the keys, and where things can go wrong.
Talking points are yours: each stop has a blank line for them.

## Before class (30 minutes early)

- [ ] Copy `game/dist/local.html` and `game/dist/phaser.min.js` into one folder on the desktop, and the same two files onto a USB stick. Double-click `local.html`: it runs with no network.
- [ ] Open it in Chrome on the presenting laptop. Use the local copy, not the web link, unless the network is good. (GitHub Pages can take 10 minutes to show a new build.)
- [ ] On the title: **Presenter** on. That turns on immunity, turns flashing down, stops the shake, and sets the music to 2 of 5 and the effects to 4 of 5.
- [ ] On the title: **New game**, twice (it asks). This clears your rehearsal: lines you heard, how-to slips, bodies. Without it some lines stay silent in class.
- [ ] Plug in the projector. Check where the sound goes (HDMI sound may go to a weak projector speaker). Bring a small speaker.
- [ ] Press one key before you start talking: browsers start sound only after a key or a click.
- [ ] Check the levels in the show, not on the title: press **0**, then **P**: music 2, effects 4. **P** again. Turn effects up if the room is big; the clicks and the strike carry the story.
- [ ] **U** for full screen (or Full screen on the title). Esc leaves full screen, so in full screen use **P** for the menu.
- [ ] Darken the room. Check the bedroom (key **6**): it is lit only in red and projectors wash out dark scenes. Projector on a game or cinema mode.
- [ ] Have the screen recording of a full run ready as a fallback. If the game fails, play it and talk over it.
- [ ] Rehearse the route once on this laptop and browser. Then **P**, **Title screen**, **New game** again. The title clears the address, so **Begin** starts at the opening.

## Keys for tonight

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **0** to **9** | Jump to a part, with its title card: 0 opening, 1 ep 1, 2 satellite 1, 3 ep 2, 4 satellite 2, 5 ep 3, 6 ep 4, 7 kids' room, 8 Moon, 9 backstage |
| **H** | Hold the picture to talk. No menu over it, sound ducks. H again goes on |
| **N** | Skip ahead one section per press (or solves a satellite screen). An episode has 4 to 7 sections, so keep pressing. In presenter mode it always works |
| **I** | Immunity off and on. Off: she can die (the small tag under the lives reads CAN DIE) |
| **Backspace** | Start this take again |
| **Enter** | Next line (the bedroom, backstage and the playroom wait for it) |
| **P** | Pause menu (presenter, big captions, music and effects levels, title screen) |
| **M** | Mute at once (works while held with H too) |
| **U** | Full screen on and off |
| **F5** | A refresh goes straight back into the part you were in (presenter mode) |

Endings and give-up mode: title screen, **Chapters**, right column. In presenter mode all five endings are open there.

## If something goes wrong

- Stuck or confused: **N**. Wrong part: the number key. A part looks broken: **Backspace**, then the number key.
- Frozen or black: **F5**. In presenter mode it comes straight back into the same part (press **U** again for full screen).
- Sound gone: **M** (muted?), then the pause menu levels, then the laptop's output device.
- Game will not load: the USB copy. Then the recording.
- A how-to slip pops up: any key closes it. A number key closes it and jumps in one press.
- Back to the title (**P**, **Title screen**): **Continue** goes back to the part you were in; **From the start** begins at the opening.
