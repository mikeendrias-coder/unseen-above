SAT Routing puzzles

Prototype for batch 1. Placeholder art, mechanics only.
  • Draw: press on 1 and drag through every square, passing the numbers in order.
  • Undo: drag back along your line, or press any square on it to cut back to there.
  • Cube: arrow keys or the buttons under the cube turn it. Press the end of your line to keep going.
  • Stuck: "Show me" finishes the route from where you are. R resets.
